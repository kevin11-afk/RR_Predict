How pressure is detected

A delivery is tagged when at least one of these match-state conditions applies.

CHASE STRESS

Second innings, 5+ overs elapsed; required rate ≥ 9 and ≥ current rate + 2.5.

WICKET STRESS

Five or more wickets lost after the first five overs.

DEATH + COLLAPSE

Final five overs with four or more wickets already lost.

Revised innings lengths are used where supplied. This is a transparent proxy, not a complete measure of match importance or psychological pressure.

Scoring methodology

Percentile-based comparative indices. Pressure rates are shrunk toward each player’s overall baseline using a 100-ball prior.

BATTER INDEX · 100

25% pressure strike rate · 20% pressure average · 20% strike-rate lift · 15% boundary frequency · 20% pressure-ball exposure.

BOWLER INDEX · 100

25% pressure economy (lower is better) · 25% wicket rate · 15% dot-ball rate · 15% economy improvement · 20% pressure-ball exposure.

ELIGIBILITY FILTER

At least 100 pressure balls, 8 pressure matches and 250 total legal balls for challenge rankings.
